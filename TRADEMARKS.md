software (see [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md)); the name and logo are not covered by

- say truthfully that your work is based on, uses or is compatible with Linwas;
- keep the attribution line required by NOTICE.md;
- use the name to refer to the original project (articles, reviews, tutorials, packages of the

**You may not, without written permission:**
- name a modified version "Linwas" or a confusingly similar name, or use the Linwas logo for it;
- suggest that a modified version is the official Linwas or is endorsed by its author;
- use the name or logo in a company, product or domain name.

Distributions that package the unmodified program (or only change packaging and integration) may
keep the name. Questions and permission requests: open an issue on the project page.
